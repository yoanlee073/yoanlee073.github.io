01 — About

01 — 關於我

I deliver a track record of duality: a physicist unveiling physical mechanisms and scaling laws, and an engineer designing and integrating products hands-on.

At ASML, I build physics-informed controllers and multiphysics analysis tools for EUV projection optics, spanning optics, thermal control, and optimization. I hold a PhD in fluid dynamics from the University of Twente, where I measured complex flows with optics and algorithms. Before that, I designed and developed wearables as a product development engineer at Garmin. I trained as a mechanical engineer at National Taiwan University.

我的職涯在兩種身份間流轉 - 科學家的一面著迷於的現象背後的物理機制，而同時也是親手設計、整合產品的工程師。

在艾斯摩爾（ASML），我為 EUV 投影光學開發物理控制演算法與多物理耦合分析工具，涵蓋光學、熱控制與最佳化。 我在荷蘭特文特大學取得流體力學博士學位，以光學量測與演算法解析複雜流動。 在此之前，我在Garmin擔任智慧穿戴機構工程師。 畢業於台大機械系/所。

Industry and academia, in rotation

在產業與學界之間輪轉

My path zigzags between the bench and the factory floor on purpose. Mechanical engineering training at NTU (Taiwan) and product development work at Garmin built a bias toward things that have to actually ship. A PhD in the Physics of Fluids group at the University of Twente pulled the other way — toward understanding thouroughly a mechanism behind observation, and trying to derive scaling law for the underlying physics . I'm currently back on the industry side at ASML, surfing on the front of AI booming, changing the world, one nanometer at a time. Full record on LinkedIn and Google Scholar.

我的職涯在實驗室跟業界之間來回穿梭。台大機械系的訓練,加上 在 Garmin 做產品開發的那幾年,養成了我對產品的可製造性和開發時程的重視。 後來去Twente大學流體物理研究室念博士,又把我拉往另一個方向 —— 探究觀察到的物理現象背後的機制, 並找出物理量之間的數學關係。現在我又回到產業界,在 ASML,站在 AI 浪潮最前線, 以奈米為單位改變世界。完整經歷可以看我的 LinkedIn 與 Google Scholar。

Industry

ASML — Design Engineer

Mirror heating control, low-NA EUV projection optics

Design engineer for mirror heating control on ASML's EUV projection optics — the multilayer mirror systems that focus EUV light onto the wafer. Repeated exposure pulses heat the optics by millikelvins, enough to distort the image by nanometers. Day-to-day work is data analysis and model-calibration algorithms that keep that thermal drift under control, plus evaluating new control and optimization strategies.

Garmin — Mechanical Engineer

Design for manufacturing, product development

Mechanical engineer focused on design-for-manufacturing and product development — turning design intent into smart wearables on your wrist built at volume. Details on LinkedIn.

01

Turbulent mixing and phase transition

紊流混合與相變

PhD · University of Twente, Physics of Fluids · 2019–2023 · with Detlef Lohse & Sander Huisman

博士 · 特文特大學流體物理研究群 · 2019–2023 · 指導教授 Detlef Lohse 與 Sander Huisman

How turbulence drives a liquid mixture past the point where droplets nucleate. I studied turbulent jets mixing a ternary solvent system, and tracked concentration fields near the turbulent/non-turbulent interface in confined and unsteady jets. Thesis: Mixing and Solvent Exchange in Turbulent Jet Flows. Video: APS DFD Gallery of Fluid Motion.

研究紊流如何把液體混合物推過成核點、生成微液滴。我研究紊流噴流中三元溶劑系統的混合，並追蹤受限與非穩態噴流中，紊流/非紊流界面附近的濃度場。博士論文：Mixing and Solvent Exchange in Turbulent Jet Flows。影片：APS DFD Gallery of Fluid Motion。

Featured paper

代表論文

Lee, Y.-A., Sun, C., Huisman, S. G., & Lohse, D. (2022). Micro-droplet nucleation through solvent exchange in a turbulent buoyant jet. Journal of Fluid Mechanics, 943, A11.

02

Phase change on engineered surfaces

工程表面上的相變

Master's · National Taiwan University · 2014–2016 · collaborations 2020–2021

碩士 · 國立台灣大學 · 2014–2016 · 後續合作 2020–2021

How surface design changes condensation and boiling heat transfer. I built surfaces with interlaced wettability, modified with nanoparticles, and measured how they perform in condensation, including the effect of surface orientation. Later collaborations extended this to boiling on graphene coatings and to self-propelled droplets on superheated surfaces.

研究表面設計如何改變凝結與沸騰熱傳。我製作以奈米顆粒改質的交錯潤濕性表面，量測其凝結熱傳表現，以及表面方向的影響。後續合作延伸到石墨烯塗層表面的沸騰熱傳，以及過熱表面上的自驅動液滴。

Featured paper

代表論文

Lee, Y.-A., Kuo, L.-S., et al. (2016). Orientation effects of nanoparticle-modified surfaces with interlaced wettability on condensation heat transfer. Applied Thermal Engineering, 98, 1054–1060.
